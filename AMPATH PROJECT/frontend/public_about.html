About AMPATH

AMPATH is a comprehensive healthcare system dedicated to improving access to quality medical services, research, and community health programs. We connect patients, healthcare workers, and researchers through a modern digital health platform.

Who We Are

Healthcare Services

We provide outpatient, inpatient, emergency, and specialized medical care across all departments.

Research & Innovation

We support medical research, clinical trials, and data-driven healthcare innovation.

Community Outreach

We reach communities with health education, outreach programs, and preventive care services.

Digital Health System

A modern hospital management system connecting patients, staff, and departments in real-time.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality healthcare services through innovation, research, and community-centered care systems that improve lives.

Our Vision

To become a leading digital healthcare network in Africa, offering integrated medical, research, and training services.
