Our Medical Services

AMPATH provides comprehensive healthcare services including emergency care, diagnostics, specialized treatment, research, and community health programs across all departments.

What We Offer

Emergency Services

24/7 emergency response with fast medical attention and ambulance services.

Outpatient Care

Consultations with doctors, routine checkups, and follow-up care services.

Inpatient Services

Comfortable admission wards with full monitoring and medical support.

Laboratory Services

Accurate diagnostic tests, blood work, and pathology services.

Radiology

X-ray, CT scan, MRI, and imaging services for diagnosis and treatment.

Pharmacy

Access to prescribed medication and professional pharmaceutical advice.

Maternity Care

Pregnancy care, delivery services, and postnatal support for mothers.

Mental Health

Counseling, therapy, and psychiatric support services.

How to Access Services

1. Register as a patient
2. Book an appointment online or visit reception
3. Get consultation from a doctor
4. Proceed for tests or treatment
5. Receive medication or follow-up care
