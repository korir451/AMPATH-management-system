About AMPATH

AMPATH is a healthcare partnership dedicated to improving access to quality medical services, training healthcare professionals, and supporting community health programs.

Our Mission

To provide sustainable, accessible, and high-quality healthcare services through collaboration, research, and community engagement.

Our Vision

To be a leading model of integrated healthcare delivery and medical education in Africa.

Core Values

  • Integrity
  • Compassion
  • Innovation
  • Collaboration
  • Excellence

What We Do

AMPATH provides outpatient and inpatient care, HIV/AIDS programs, maternal health services, medical research, training, and community outreach programs across multiple regions.
